If you are in danger right now

Call 911. For confidential help with abuse, contact the National Domestic Violence Hotline: call 1-800-799-7233 or text START to 88788. If you are thinking about suicide or self-harm, call or text 988.

1. What we are, and what we are not

We offer Christian marriage coaching and biblical teaching. We are not licensed therapists, counselors, doctors or attorneys [CLIENT TO CONFIRM CREDENTIALS], and our coaching is not a substitute for professional mental-health care, medical care or legal advice. When a situation calls for professional help, we will tell you, and help you find it.

2. Abuse is never God's design

Scripture never asks anyone to stay in danger. Our teaching on submission, respect and reconciliation does not apply to physical, sexual, emotional, financial or spiritual abuse. Couples coaching and group settings are not appropriate when abuse is present. They can make things more dangerous.

If you share that abuse is happening, we will:

  • Believe you and take it seriously.
  • Talk with you privately, never in front of your spouse or the group.
  • Connect you with trained domestic-violence advocates and resources.
  • Pause joint coaching until it is safe.

If you're worried your spouse monitors your email or devices, contact us from a safe device or account.

3. Limits of confidentiality

We keep what you share private. But we may have to share information, without your permission, when:

  • Someone is at immediate risk of serious harm to themselves or others.
  • We learn about the abuse or neglect of a child, an elder, or a dependent adult. Depending on our state and role, we may be legally required to report it.
  • We are required by law, such as by a court order or subpoena.

Recordings related to a safety concern may be kept longer than usual if the law requires it.

4. Safety in group sessions

  • Facilitators can move anyone to a private room or end a breakout if a conversation becomes unsafe.
  • Harassment, threats, contempt or sexual content toward others means immediate removal.
  • You never have to share anything you're not comfortable sharing.
  • Cohort members must be 18 or older.

5. Reporting a concern

If anything in a session, from a facilitator or from another member, made you feel unsafe, email . Concerns go directly to ministry leadership and are handled confidentially.
