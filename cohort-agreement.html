Our cohort is a place to be honest about your marriage. That only works if you can trust how your words, face and voice are handled. Here's our promise, in plain language. By joining a cohort session, you agree to it.

1. Every session is recorded

All cohort sessions are recorded, including the main teaching time, Q&A and breakout groups. We'll remind you at the start of every session. We record so that we can:

  • Let cohort members re-watch the teaching portions they missed.
  • Improve our teaching and train our facilitators.
  • Create short teaching clips of Terrye's own teaching to share publicly.

2. We will never share your face or voice without asking first

This is our firmest promise. Before any recording that includes your face, your voice, your name or your story is shared outside the cohort (on social media, our website, ads, or anywhere else), we will:

  1. Reach out to you personally and show you exactly what we'd like to share and where.
  2. Get your written permission, from both spouses if you both appear.
  3. Respect a "no" completely, with no pressure and no follow-up asking again.

Silence is not permission. If we don't hear back, we don't share. You can withdraw permission later, and we'll take the content down from the accounts we control.

3. Breakout groups stay private

Breakout groups may be recorded for safety and quality, but breakout recordings are never used for public content and are not included in member replays. Only ministry leadership can access them.

4. Your choices in every session

  • You may keep your camera off and use a first name or initials as your display name.
  • You can listen without sharing. Participating is always optional.
  • You can ask us to edit you out of a member replay.
  • You can ask us to delete recordings of you, unless we're required to keep them for legal or safety reasons (see our Safety Policy).

5. What we ask of you

  • What's shared here, stays here. Don't repeat other couples' stories or identifying details outside the cohort.
  • No personal recordings. Don't record, screenshot or stream sessions yourself.
  • Speak with grace. Be honest without shaming your spouse or anyone else.
  • Don't counsel other couples privately as if you were a coach. Point them back to the group or to Terrye.

We may remove anyone who breaks this agreement, to protect the group.

6. How recordings are stored

Recordings are kept in private, access-controlled storage. Only ministry leadership can access them. Teaching-only replays are kept for [12 months — CLIENT TO CONFIRM], and breakout recordings for [90 days — CLIENT TO CONFIRM], after which they're deleted.

7. Questions or requests

Email about anything related to recordings, permissions or removal. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
